Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how KYROBOT ("we") handles data in OpenJarvis, our internal publishing tool. OpenJarvis connects to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram so we can publish our own videos to our own accounts. It is not offered to the public. The only accounts connected to it are KYROBOT's own.

Who we are

KYROBOT is run by Jani Männistö, a private individual based in Finland. Contact: werstas.ai@gmail.com.

What data we access

When a KYROBOT account signs in with Google, OpenJarvis asks for permission to:

  • see the account's basic profile (name, email address, profile picture),
  • manage the account's YouTube channel: upload videos and set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails,
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports.

When a KYROBOT account signs in with TikTok, OpenJarvis asks for permission to:

  • see the account's basic profile and public statistics (display name, profile picture, follower and like counts),
  • see the list of the account's own videos,
  • upload and publish videos to the account.

When a KYROBOT account signs in with Instagram, OpenJarvis asks for permission to:

  • see the account's basic profile (username, profile picture),
  • publish posts and reels to the account,
  • read and reply to comments on the account's own posts,
  • read insights (reach and engagement) of the account's own posts.

How we use it

  • Profile data is used to show which account is connected.
  • Publishing access is used only to publish content that we have made and approved ourselves.
  • Analytics, statistics and insights are used only to see how our own content performs.
  • Comment access is used only to read and answer comments on our own posts.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and channel details are stored on a computer that KYROBOT controls. They are not stored on third-party servers by us.
  • We do not sell, rent or share user data from Google, TikTok or Instagram with anyone.
  • We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.
  • Data is kept only as long as we need it for publishing. When we disconnect a channel, we delete its stored tokens.

Limited Use

OpenJarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

OpenJarvis uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove OpenJarvis's access at any time: for Google from the Google security settings page, for TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions in the TikTok app, and for Instagram under Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites in the Instagram app. You can also ask us to delete stored data by emailing the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
